Selecta AI

Select text in any app, press one shortcut, and AI fixes it, translates it, answers it, or turns it into a clean prompt.

  • Search every AI chat. All your ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini conversations in one place, searchable even when you only vaguely remember what was said.
  • A companion in any app. Press Ctrl+Shift+Alt+Space from email, chat, code editors or the browser.
  • Prompts, translation and rewrites on a shortcut, with the result placed back where your text was.
  • Your data stays on your device. No account, no tracking.

About

A small studio for focused AI tools

Selecta Labs is an independent software studio. We started with a simple gesture: select, then act. Highlight text anywhere and let AI do the next step, without copying, pasting or switching tabs.

That idea became Selecta AI, and it shapes everything we make: tools that respect your privacy, work with the AI you already trust, and stay out of your way.
